Frequently asked questions

Purchases and Bouquets

I bought something but it did not arrive

Open the in-game Store and choose Restore Purchases. Non-consumable purchases return on devices using the same Apple Account. A purchase waiting for Ask to Buy arrives after approval.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes every purchase and refund. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and select the purchase.

Do purchases make a career stronger?

Every functional purchase is disclosed before confirmation and removes the career's Competitive status. Useful catalog items also have a play-earned route through Bouquets.

Careers and saves

Where is my career saved?

Career and account data are stored locally on your device. Palmares uses atomic replacement, integrity checks and rotating backups to protect saves. Deleting the app removes local data.

The game restored an older save — what happened?

The newest save could not be read, so Palmares recovered the latest valid backup rather than discarding the career. You may need to replay recent progress.

How do I delete a career?

Open Careers from Rider or Settings. Deletion requires two confirmations and names the affected rider because it cannot be undone.

Playing

Does Palmares require an internet connection?

No. The career plays offline. Internet access is only needed for Apple services such as browsing or restoring Store purchases.

Which languages are available?

Palmares supports Danish, German, English, Spanish, Finnish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese (Portugal), Russian, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese.

Is Palmares training or medical advice?

No. Palmares is a game. Training, nutrition, supplements, injury and health information are fictionalized simulation systems, not medical or coaching guidance.
