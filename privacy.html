Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 16 October 2026

This policy explains how Palmares, the cycling career game for iPhone and iPad (the “app”), and this website handle information. Questions: palmares.career@gmail.com.

In short
  • Palmares has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • There is no Palmares account, login or developer-operated server.
  • Career and account data can sync privately through your Apple iCloud account when you enable iCloud sync.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases; the developer never receives payment-card details.

1. Game data and iCloud

To play, Palmares stores career saves, rider names and looks, results, journal entries, trophies, achievements, settings, Bouquet balance, unlocks, owned products and delivered transaction identifiers locally. Saves use integrity checks, atomic replacement and rotating backups for recovery.

When iCloud sync is enabled, career saves are mirrored to the app's private CloudKit database and account metadata is synchronized through iCloud key-value storage. This data is associated with your Apple Account, is available only to you and counts toward your iCloud storage. You can disable career sync in Palmares Settings. Palmares has no developer-operated account or server.

2. App Store purchases

Optional products are sold through Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment. The app receives the product and transaction information needed to deliver, restore or revoke content, and records delivery locally so the same transaction is not granted twice. Apple's handling is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Game Center

Palmares may use Apple's Game Center entitlement for achievements. Game Center is controlled by your Apple Account and Apple's settings. The app does not operate its own social profile service or receive your payment information.

4. Sharing

If you export a save, poster or share card, it leaves the app only when you choose a destination using Apple's system interfaces. The receiving app or service applies its own privacy policy.

5. What Palmares does not access

  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or Health data.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

6. Children

Palmares does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. App Store age-rating answers must always match the released version.

7. Deleting data

Delete an individual career from Careers in Rider or Settings; the app requires two confirmations and also removes its CloudKit copy when iCloud sync is enabled. Delete the app to remove local data. You can manage iCloud data through your Apple Account settings. Because the developer holds no Palmares account data, there is no developer server-side account to delete.

8. Support e-mails

If you write to palmares.career@gmail.com, your address and message are used only to answer the request and troubleshoot the issue. Do not send passwords, payment information or sensitive health information.

9. This website

This is a static website without cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or forms. GitHub Pages may process technical information for security and reliability under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Palmares developer does not receive or use GitHub's server logs.

10. Changes

If a future version handles information differently, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release, and the effective date will change.
